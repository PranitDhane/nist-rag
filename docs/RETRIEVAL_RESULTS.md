## Known weaknesses and limitations

- **Two answerable paraphrases fall below the threshold and would be marked not relevant:** c29 "How can an organization safely retire a generative model it no longer needs?" (best score 0.0054, the GOVERN 1.7 table is rank 2) and c31 "How should we check that a language model treats different demographic groups fairly?" (0.020). The next phase's query-retry step is the place to recover these.
- **The threshold is tuned on a small set** (33 answerable, 10 unanswerable); the 0.073-0.240 plateau is real on these cases but not a guarantee. The highest unanswerable scores belong to the questions closest to the corpus (quantum computing 0.073, NIST CSF Identify function 0.065).
- **Reranker input limit:** 27 of 745 (query, candidate) pairs (3.6%) exceed 512 tokens and are cut; the tail of long table chunks is not seen by the reranker.
- **`EnsembleRetriever` hides fusion scores and which retriever found a chunk.** `retrieval.diagnostics` recomputes weighted RRF independently (it reproduced the ensemble order on every checked query).
- **`BM25Retriever` pads results with zero-score chunks** when fewer than k chunks match (for `GV-1.7-001`, 1 real hit and 9 arbitrary chunks). They enter fusion as if relevant; the reranker discards them. Present in 1 of 43 evaluation queries.
- `langchain-community` is flagged as being sunset (DeprecationWarning); only `BM25Retriever` is taken from it.
- Sibling expansion applies to split **tables** only (largest `parts_total` is 2), so the largest possible result is 10 chunks. Prose chunks also carry `parts_total > 1` but are never expanded.
